about

Thomas English is a journalist and editor based in Washington, D.C., covering politics, policy, and the stories that shape how Americans understand power. His reporting has appeared in national publications and been cited by major outlets across the political spectrum.

Before turning to journalism full-time, Thomas developed a sharp eye for narrative structure and an ear for the language of institutions — skills that inform his ability to translate complex policy debates into clear, compelling prose. He brings a deliberate editorial sensibility to every piece, whether it's a breaking news dispatch or a long-form investigation.

When he's not writing, he's probably reading something printed before 1970, arguing about typefaces, or walking through D.C. pretending it's a European capital.
